Approach

Four stages, each with a clear end, and a feedback loop back to the start.

  1. Discover

    The first week

    Whiteboards and awkward questions until the real problem shows up.

  2. Prototype

    Days, not quarters

    A working proof-of-concept on your real stack, so the decision is made on evidence.

  3. Architect

    The boring parts, done properly

    Boundaries, decisions written down, and a platform that outlives the project.

  4. Scale

    So the team runs it without me

    Tooling, pipelines and a hand-over to your engineers.

About

I work end to end, from early problem framing to the tooling, platforms and delivery details that make software work in the real world. Eleven years in, I still believe great systems come from sweating the small details until they disappear.

Away from work I build things slowly. The 6502 in the photo is the best teacher I know for the things cloud consoles hide. The bonsai is a reminder that the systems worth building are the ones you are still tending long after the launch. I also explore DIY builds, biotech and retro computing.

Based in Kolkata, India.
